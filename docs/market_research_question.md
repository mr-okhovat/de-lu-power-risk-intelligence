# DE-LU Market Research Question

## Established background

High residual load and low wind/PV generation are known to be associated with
higher German day-ahead power prices through merit-order and scarcity
mechanisms.

The current project therefore does not treat that broad relationship as a new
finding.

## Candidate research gap

Under what observable pre-auction conditions does a high-residual / low-
renewable delivery hour transition from an ordinary high-price hour into a
scarcity-like day-ahead price-tail event?

Potential explanatory groups to assess later:

- forecast residual load level and forecast uncertainty;
- planned and unplanned conventional availability;
- gas, coal and EU ETS cost conditions;
- cross-zonal capacity, imports and coupled-market conditions;
- demand/weather stress and calendar structure.

## Current evidence boundary

The existing 2020–2024 public-data result is an ex-post association screen.
It does not identify a causal mechanism, pre-auction information set, trading
signal or independently estimated low-renewable-share effect.

## Next research stage

Complete a primary literature, regulator and market-design scan before adding
new data fields or formulating an external industry question.
