# Market Discovery Confirmation Gate

## Purpose

Confirm whether the observed ex-post association between realised DE-LU
fundamentals and same-delivery-hour day-ahead price stress remains credible
after time-dependence-aware checks.

## Checks

1. Monthly block bootstrap of the controlled composite effect.
2. Leave-one-year-out stability.
3. Weekly circular placebo shifts of the high-price outcome within each month.

## Boundary

This remains ex-post explanatory research. It does not establish causality,
forecastability, tradability or pre-auction information availability.

## Decision

The finding remains a candidate for mechanism and literature work only if:

- the monthly block-bootstrap interval remains positive;
- every leave-one-year-out estimate remains positive;
- the observed aligned effect exceeds the weekly placebo effects.

Otherwise, retain the result as descriptive and do not formulate an industry
question around it.
