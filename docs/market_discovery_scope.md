# Market Discovery Scope

## Immediate research question

Does the realised DE-LU system state show explanatory association with
same-delivery-hour day-ahead price stress after calendar and regime controls?

## Explicit boundary

This phase is ex-post explanatory research. It is not an ex-ante trading or
forecasting claim.

The price series represents the day-ahead auction price for the delivery hour.
Current fundamentals are realised load and realised renewable generation for
that delivery hour. A later tradability assessment requires forecast data and
verified publication timing available before the day-ahead auction.

## Current build

Build and audit a clean hourly DE-LU day-ahead price panel for
2020-01-01 to 2024-12-31 before any market hypothesis is tested.
